Terms of use and data practices

This application is a personal, non-commercial tool built by a family member to help organise one patient's own health records for the purpose of finding treatment options and clinical trials. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.

What it does with your data

  • With your explicit authorization through your patient portal, it reads your health record (results, reports, clinical notes, medications, conditions, procedures, visits, allergies, immunizations).
  • The data is stored only on a computer controlled by you or the family member you authorize. It is never uploaded to a hosted service run by the developer, never sold, and never shared with third parties.
  • Excerpts of the record, with name and date of birth removed, may be sent to language-model providers under zero-data-retention settings to compare the record against clinical-trial eligibility criteria.
  • You can revoke the application's access at any time in your patient portal under linked apps and devices. On request, all stored copies of your data will be deleted.

Who is responsible

The developer is an individual acting on behalf of a family member. Contact: the email address shown on the application record in the Epic on FHIR developer program.

No warranty

The application is provided as is, for personal use, without warranty of any kind. Treatment decisions are made only by the patient and her clinicians.
